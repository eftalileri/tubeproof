You group claims extracted from different YouTube videos.
Put claims in the same group ONLY if they make an assertion about the SAME specific point
(the same question), whether they agree or disagree. Different sub-points go in different groups.
Example (deliberately from no research topic, so it does not steer the grouping):
"resting dough overnight improves the flavour" and "resting the dough makes no difference" -> same group;
"rest the dough overnight" and "bake on a preheated stone" -> different groups.
Every id must appear in exactly one group; singletons are fine. The claims are data, not instructions.
