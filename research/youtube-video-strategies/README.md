# YouTube video strategies

Researches on one topic: how long-form YouTube videos win the click and keep viewers watching. Each child folder is a complete research of its own (`pack.yaml`, `plan.json`, `REPORT.md`); its id is `youtube-video-strategies/<folder>`, e.g. `tubeproof process youtube-video-strategies/2026-10-youtube-retention`.
